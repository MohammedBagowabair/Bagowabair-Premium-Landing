# Marketing page

Mode: Persuade.

Audience: an English-speaking business owner, startup, consultant, or professional deciding whether to hire one developer. They need to see the offer, the working relationship, and real demos, then open Upwork.

Constraints: static page, no invented proof, primary action is “Hire me on Upwork”.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a morning edition with one lead story — Mohammed, the work, and a way to hire him — and it refuses the freelancer template of a centered slogan, a row of equal service cards, and borrowed credibility.

OWN-WORLD: A teal nameplate and lead field (#143F46) on cool stone paper (#E7EEEF), ink (#102226), and one amber thread (#E3B23C) that appears only on the teal. Schibsted Grotesk throughout. The thread is the only chromatic event (neon-circuit raise). Every block is a fact, a service, or an action (teletext raise). No invented numbers (six-pack raise).

STORY: The visitor reads who Mohammed is, what he builds, how a project moves, and three live demos labeled as concepts, then hires him on Upwork. They believe the work is done directly with him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A sticky teal nameplate with the amber thread along the top, the full name, section links, and the hire action. Below, a left-aligned headline at display scale owns the field, a short deck and the hire action sit under it, and a browser frame of the Artvue demo meets the stone paper on its lower edge.

FORM: Morning edition, seventh on the grounded list (after the studio wall, the proposal folio, the concierge desk, the sample library, the gallery, and the letter). Seed key ed96a0fb. Telop raise: one headline, no second display line. HyperCard raise: each section is one idea and one action. Crank raise: one motion, the thread drawing on load and again as the process rail.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
