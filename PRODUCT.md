# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. GSAP may load from a CDN. No build step, no framework, no WordPress. Deployed as-is to GitHub Pages from the repository root, so asset URLs are relative and the site must work at `https://mohammedbagowabair.github.io/Bagowabair-Premium-Landing/`.

## Users

International English-speaking business owners, startups, consultants, and professionals who need a website or a custom web application and prefer to hire one developer directly.

## Product Purpose

A single-page marketing site for Mohammed Bagowabair, an independent software developer based in Yemen. Success is a visitor who understands what he builds, that they work with him directly, and who follows the primary action to hire him on Upwork.

## Positioning

Clients work directly with Mohammed, not an agency. He designs and builds professional business websites, modern personal-brand websites, portfolios, landing pages, and custom web applications, and he redesigns and modernizes existing sites. Stack he uses: HTML/CSS/JS, React/Next.js, .NET / Blazor, ASP.NET Core APIs.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from search, a portfolio link, GitHub, LinkedIn, or Upwork. They compare him with agencies and template shops. The page is the pitch; the hire happens on Upwork.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One static page. Semantic landmarks. Mobile-first from 360px through 1440px and wider.
- Primary call to action on every major section: "Hire me on Upwork", linking to `https://www.upwork.com/freelancers/~011e87722b96f4b0fb`, opening in a new tab with `rel="noopener"`.
- Also link the live portfolio `https://www.bagowabair.com/`, GitHub `https://github.com/mohammedbagowabair`, and LinkedIn `https://www.linkedin.com/in/bagowabair`.
- Selected work may link three live demo projects and must be labeled demo or concept projects, never client work:
  - `https://mohammedbagowabair.github.io/active-link-automation/`
  - `https://mohammedbagowabair.github.io/Artvue-Art-Marketplace-Website/`
  - `https://mohammedbagowabair.github.io/Noor-Ladies-Salon-Website/`
- A short process (discovery, design, build, launch) is appropriate.
- Motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Lighthouse performance, accessibility, best practices, and SEO each 90 or above.
- Do not invent statistics, project or client counts, satisfaction percentages, testimonials, client logos, or pricing.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Mohammed Bagowabair. Independent software developer, Yemen. Voice is premium, calm, and confident, at the craft level of a top-tier product site. Visual study set, binding as reference rather than as a copy: useorigin.com, cosmos.so, sanalabs.com, duna.com, giga.ai, superhuman.com, apple.com, dovetail.com.

## Evidence on Hand

Services, stack, direct-hire positioning, three live demo URLs, and the portfolio, GitHub, LinkedIn, and Upwork links listed above. No testimonials, client logos, case metrics, or prices are on hand, and none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Show the work and the working relationship; do not borrow credibility the evidence does not support.
- One developer, one conversation, from first brief to launch.
- The page should feel considered and quiet enough that the offer is obvious in the first screen.
- Every claim on the page must be checkable against the links or the stated services.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG contrast AA, visible focus, semantic landmarks, alternative text on meaningful images, and a reduced-motion experience that keeps all content available without animation.
